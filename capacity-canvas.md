## 8. Decision

At the end of the pilot, choose one:

- **Scale:** operational and business measures meet the agreed thresholds.
- **Redesign:** the value hypothesis remains plausible, but adoption, workflow design, data, or controls need work.
- **Stop:** the evidence does not justify continued investment or the risk remains unacceptable.
